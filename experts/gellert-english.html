Expert on Hungarian Politics and EU Affairs

Strategic engagement professional specializing in Hungarian foreign policy, civil liberties, and EU affairs. Currently based in Washington, DC, Márton Gellért brings experience spanning EU institutions, the private sector, think tanks, and trade associations. His expertise encompasses policy analysis, strategic communications, stakeholder engagement, event management, and coalition building, with a particular focus on Hungary-EU relations, broader EU affairs and transatlantic cooperation.

Gellért holds a bachelor's degree in international relations from the University of Sussex, and a master's degree in international development management from The University of Manchester.
