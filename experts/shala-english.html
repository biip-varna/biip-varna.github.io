Expert on National Security, Intelligence and Geopolitics

Drizan Shala is a security and defense analyst specializing in national security, intelligence, geopolitics, and strategic affairs, with a particular focus on the Western Balkans. He has extensive experience in strategic analysis, security policy, defense affairs, hybrid threats, military developments, organized crime, and regional security dynamics.

His professional work focuses on assessing emerging security challenges, geopolitical developments, intelligence-related risks, institutional resilience, and strategic developments affecting Kosovo and the wider Western Balkans. He combines policy-oriented research with practical security analysis, translating complex regional developments into clear strategic assessments and actionable insights.

Dr. Shala has experience in executive leadership, organizational management, strategic planning, institutional development, and cooperation with governmental, security, academic, and international stakeholders. He regularly contributes to public and professional discussions on national security, defense, intelligence, geopolitics, and regional stability.

His areas of expertise include National Security, Intelligence Analysis, Geopolitics, Defense and Security Policy, Hybrid Warfare, Strategic Analysis, Western Balkans Security, and Institutional Resilience.
