Expert on Türkiye, the Balkans, and European Affairs

Berrin Yeshil is an expert in the field of international relations. She graduated with a degree in International Relations from Trakya University (Türkiye), where she developed her interests in foreign policy, international security, and European integration.

She completed an internship at the Diplomatic Institute of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Bulgaria, where she contributed to the preparation of analytical reports, media overviews, reports, and the organization of international events.

Her main professional interests are related to the policies of the European Union, NATO, and Euro-Atlantic security, the relations between the European Union and Türkiye, migration processes, cybersecurity, and digital policies. During her studies, she prepared academic papers on topics related to the relations between the European Union and Türkiye, migration processes, and cultural identity. She has a published article dedicated to key concepts in international relations in an edition of the Department of International Relations.

She is fluent in Bulgarian, Turkish, English, and Russian. In her professional activities, she strives for an objective, independent, and evidence-based approach to the analysis of international processes.
