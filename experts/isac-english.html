Expert on Romania, Moldova and Foreign Policy

Mihai Isac is a political analyst and media professional with almost two decades of experience in the Romanian media. He is currently a senior editor at the news agency KARADENIZ PRESS and a permanent guest on Global Impact, the weekly foreign-policy programme of TVR Moldova in Chișinău.

He holds a bachelor’s degree in Administrative Sciences from the Faculty of Public Administration at the National School of Political and Administrative Studies (SNSPA), as well as a degree in Political Science from the Faculty of Political Science at the University of Bucharest.

As a political analyst, he collaborates regularly with media outlets in Bulgaria, Turkey, Romania, Ukraine, the Republic of Moldova, Italy, Serbia and Spain.

His areas of expertise include Romanian and Moldovan politics and foreign policy analysis.
