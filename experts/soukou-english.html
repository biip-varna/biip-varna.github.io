Expert on Western Balkans and EU Enlargement

Evelina Soukou holds a BA in Political Science and Public Administration from the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens and an MA in Peace, Security and Development from the Faculty of Political Sciences at the University of Belgrade.

Her academic and analytical interests centre on the Western Balkans, particularly post-conflict societies, political accountability, nationalism and collective memory. Her work explores how historical legacies and contemporary political conflicts shape relations between states and societies, with particular attention to the region’s European integration.

Her professional background includes diplomatic experience at the Public Diplomacy Office of the Embassy of Greece in Belgrade. She is currently undertaking a traineeship at the Delegation of the European Union to Montenegro, within the European Integration, Political, Press and Information Section. She has authored political analyses for Greek think tanks and publications.

She speaks Greek, English, French and Serbian.
