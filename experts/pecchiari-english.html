Expert on Communication & Stakeholder Engagement

Senior communication consultant with experience across consultancy and industry, specializing in institutional communication, geopolitics, and EU affairs. Beatrice Pecchiari brings a strategic, results-oriented approach to advocacy and stakeholder engagement in different sectors, including defence, shipbuilding, pharmaceuticals, and construction.

She holds a PhD in Italian Studies and a Master's in Geopolitics and Global Security, and completed an Executive course in Journalism and Multimedia Communication at the School of Journalism of Luiss Guido Carli University.
