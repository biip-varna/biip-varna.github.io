What participants take away

  1. Understand how think tanks turn research into policy work
  2. Critique a policy brief and make its recommendations stronger
  3. Build a credible public profile as a policy analyst
  4. Explore careers in press offices, stakeholder engagement and public affairs
  5. Connect politics and communication with civil society, partnerships and social impact
  6. Put your career questions to four BIIP professionals, in person and online
